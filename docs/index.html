About Me

Hi! I’m Yiwei, an incoming PhD student in the ELLIS PhD Program, advised by Prof. Andreas Bulling and Dr. Eric Schulz. I recently graduated from École polytechnique fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) with an MSc in Digital Humanities, after earning a bachelor’s degree in Statistics from Beijing Institute of Technology.

My research explores how cognitive science can inform more capable AI agents through socially aware world models. I am interested in how world models can represent the beliefs, intentions, and motivations of other agents, so that the system can reason about cooperation, deception, and strategic interaction. This connects to a broader question: what would it take for a machine to become genuinely socially intelligent?

Outside research, I enjoy tennis🎾, baking🍰 and photography📷. You can see some of moments captured through my lens: 1, 2, 3.

📢I'm always happy to chat about trustworthy AI, cognitive science, and human--AI collaboration. Feel free to reach out!
